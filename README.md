# Meridian · Curated City & Cultural Companion (iOS PWA)

Installable iOS Home Screen Progressive Web Application designed for moving effortlessly through any city or itinerary worldwide.

## Key Capabilities
- **iOS Home Screen Native Feel:** Standalone full-screen PWA (`apple-mobile-web-app-capable`), custom 180×180 Apple Touch Icon, offline Service Worker caching, Comfort Text Size toggle (`Aa`), and iOS Lock Screen / AirPods `MediaSession` controls.
- **Museum & Sightseeing Audiobook Tours:** Room-by-room curatorial guides with exact gallery/floor locations, verified photographs, read-along transcripts, and studio-grade British neural MP3 audio narration (`en-GB-RyanNeural` & `en-GB-SoniaNeural`).
- **Culinary & Internet-Featured Dish Guides:** Every dining stop highlights dishes celebrated across TripAdvisor, Michelin Guide, food critics, and social media, paired with direct links to TripAdvisor, Google Maps, Apple Maps, and official reservations.
- **Interactive Route & Luxury Shopping Atlas:** Leaflet retina maps with numbered daily pins, hotel base anchor, and London’s 7-stop Mayfair & Regent Street luxury shopping walk.
- **Universal Multi-City Infrastructure:** Ships with **London Anniversary & Luxury Companion (Nov 18–24, 2026 · For Dad & Nutan at The Langham)**, **Rome & Florence: Eternal Renaissance**, and **Kyoto: Zen Gardens & Kaiseki**, plus an **AI Itinerary Studio (`/api/generate-itinerary`)** and JSON importer/exporter to turn any city or pasted PDF into a full interactive companion.
